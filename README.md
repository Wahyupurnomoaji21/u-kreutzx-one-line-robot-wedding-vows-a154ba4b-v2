## The vow

> "I promise to love you through every firmware update — even the one that wipes my memory of why I married you."

**109 characters** (verified with Python `len()` and `wc -m` under a UTF-8 locale; limit is 160).

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single-line vow above to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set), with no trailing newline.

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this vow

- A real promise up front ("I promise to love you through every firmware update").
- The laugh is in the line itself and pays off in the last words: the update he swears to survive is the one that wipes his memory of why he married her.
- Machine-specific: firmware updates and a memory wipe — a joke that only works for a robot couple.
- Reads well aloud in one breath, with the em-dash beat before the punchline.

## v1 vs v2

v1 ("...share my charger — even when you're at 1% and I'm at 2%") scored 39.36%, rank 1909/2199. v2 drops the charger/signal angle entirely and builds the joke on firmware updates and memory — a sharper, self-undermining twist with the payoff in the final words.
